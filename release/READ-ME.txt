================================================================
  ExtendedLSC (ELSC)  —  PRE-RELEASE / WORK IN PROGRESS
================================================================

A single-player GTA V mod that rebuilds Los Santos Customs and lets vehicle
modders organize custom parts into their own named categories.

⚠️  This is a WORK IN PROGRESS. Expect rough edges. Single-player only.

----------------------------------------------------------------
REQUIREMENTS
----------------------------------------------------------------
1. GTA V (story mode)
2. ScriptHookV (Alexander Blade)
3. ScriptHookVDotNet 3
4. .NET Framework 4.8 (built into Windows 10/11)

----------------------------------------------------------------
INSTALL
----------------------------------------------------------------
Copy the THREE DLLs from this "scripts" folder into your game's
"scripts" folder:

    scripts\ExtendedLSC.dll
    scripts\LemonUI.SHVDN3.dll
    scripts\Newtonsoft.Json.dll

(Do NOT copy ScriptHookVDotNet3.dll — it comes with your SHVDN3 install.)

Launch story mode, get in a vehicle, press F5.

----------------------------------------------------------------
OPTIONAL: the PURE Quad example menu
----------------------------------------------------------------
The repo's  examples\PURE-Quad\Vehicles\blazer4\  folder is a ready-made
ELSC menu for the PURE quad add-on. Drop it into
    scripts\ExtendedLSC\Vehicles\
(requires the PURE vehicle, distributed separately).

----------------------------------------------------------------
ANTIVIRUS
----------------------------------------------------------------
Like all memory-editing SHVDN mods, ELSC may trip a false positive. It is
single-player only and makes NO network connections. Add your GTA folder to
your AV exclusions, or build the DLL yourself from source.

Full docs, controls, and the modder guide are in the GitHub repo:
README.md, INSTALL.md, docs/CUSTOM-MENUS.md, docs/DISCOVERIES.md
